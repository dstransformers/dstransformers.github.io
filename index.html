About the company

About D.S. Transformers

D.S. Transformers & Electrical Contractor is a transformer repair and servicing company operating from its own workshop in Mallapur, Hyderabad.

Established in 2011 and backed by more than 30 years of hands-on experience in the transformer service field, the company provides transformer repair, breakdown servicing, restoration, coil rewinding, oil filtration, gasket replacement, oil leakage rectification and maintenance services.

The Mallapur facility provides a spacious work area with dedicated machinery and CCTV surveillance for transformer repair and servicing activities.

The workshop has the capability to undertake transformer repair work up to 1 MVA, subject to inspection, transformer condition and repair feasibility.
